Who We Are

CLUB INTRODUCTION

We are a passionate community of student developers, researchers, and tech enthusiasts dedicated to exploring the frontiers of Artificial Intelligence.

From building localized Large Language Models to hosting hands-on robotics workshops, we bridge the gap between academic theory and practical, real-world tech solutions across campus.

What Drives Us

Mission & Vision

M

Our Mission

To democratize AI education by providing inclusive, hands-on learning experiences and resources. We strive to empower students from all backgrounds to build ethical, innovative technologies that solve local and global challenges.

V

Our Vision

To become a leading hub for undergraduate AI innovation, fostering a collaborative ecosystem where curiosity meets cutting-edge engineering, and where today's students evolve into tomorrow's tech leaders.
